Fire District Locator

Enter an address to determine which York County, SC fire district it is in. For best results, use a complete address including city, state, and ZIP code.

Results will appear here...

This tool uses ArcGIS geocoding services to convert addresses into GPS coordinates. These coordinates are then checked against a map of fire districts pulled from York County GIS data. No address data is stored.
