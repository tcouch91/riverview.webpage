Station 1, located at 1899 Harris Road, serves as the primary hub for the Riverview Fire Department. This station houses a versatile fleet of apparatus designed to handle a wide variety of emergencies. Key units include Engine 1, our frontline pumper; Rescue 1, equipped for technical rescues and vehicle extrication; and Tanker 1, which provides crucial water supply in areas without hydrants.

This station's strategic location allows for rapid response to the surrounding residential and commercial areas. The dedicated crews at Station 1 train rigorously to maintain readiness for fire suppression, medical emergencies, and specialized rescue operations, ensuring the safety and protection of our community.
